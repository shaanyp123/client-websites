### 1001 Residences
**Meta title:** 1001 Residences — Multifamily Plumbing, Philadelphia | Plumbing Resolution
**H1:** 1001 Residences
**Facts:** 1001 S. Broad Street, Philadelphia · 630 units · Mixed-use
renovation · $6.5M contract
**Body:** One of the largest residential projects in Philadelphia's
history: a 630-unit redevelopment with first-floor retail and rooftop
amenities. Plumbing Resolution delivered the full plumbing scope —
domestic water distribution, sanitary waste and vent, unit kitchens and
bathrooms, water-heating systems, common-area and retail infrastructure,
rooftop amenity plumbing, and project-wide fixture installation.

### Piazza Alta
**Facts:** 1099 Germantown Avenue, Philadelphia · 680 units · Ground-up
mixed-use · $6.8M contract
**Body:** A ground-up, high-density development of roughly 680 units with
first-floor retail and extensive rooftop amenities. Our scope ran from
underground sanitary systems through domestic water distribution, unit
kitchens and baths, water heating, retail plumbing infrastructure, and the
gas and plumbing systems serving rooftop and shared amenity spaces.

### The Poplar
**Facts:** 900 N. 9th Street, Philadelphia · 285 units · Adaptive reuse ·
$4.7M contract
**Body:** A historic structure transformed into a mixed-use community of
285 apartments with commercial space and extensive resident amenities.
Adaptive reuse means building new systems inside an existing frame: we
delivered residential plumbing throughout — domestic water, sanitary and
vent piping, kitchens and bathrooms, common areas, and rooftop amenity
plumbing — coordinated around the constraints of the original building.

### The Darien
**Facts:** 815 Poplar Street, Philadelphia · 212 units · Ground-up ·

**Body:** Ground-up construction of 212 multifamily units over first-floor
retail. Full plumbing construction: underground piping, sanitary waste and
vent, domestic water distribution, residential kitchens and bathrooms,
fixtures and equipment connections, and commercial/retail infrastructure
at grade.

### 650 Fairmount
**Facts:** 650 Fairmount Street, Philadelphia · 107 townhomes · Ground-up ·

**Body:** A 107-townhome new-construction development — plumbing at
neighborhood scale. Our team ran ground-up plumbing across the entire
development: domestic water, sanitary and vent systems, kitchens and
bathrooms, water heaters, and fixtures, repeated cleanly across a hundred-
plus homes on a production schedule.

### Trout National — The Reserve, Clubhouse
**Facts:** Vineland, New Jersey · Private golf club · Hospitality

## About

**Meta title:** About Plumbing Resolution Inc. | Philadelphia Commercial Plumbing
**Meta description:** Founded in 2016 by Licensed Master Plumber Jeffrey
Devine, Plumbing Resolution is a ~40-person commercial plumbing contractor
serving Pennsylvania and New Jersey.

**H1:** A contractor built on accountability

**Company story:** Plumbing Resolution was founded in 2016 with a simple
premise: complex projects don't need a bigger promise — they need a
plumbing contractor who communicates clearly, coordinates well, and owns
its scope. That premise has carried us from first projects to 2,000+
multifamily units and more than $20M in delivered contract value, on
projects ranging from $50,000 fit-outs to $6.8M ground-up developments
across Pennsylvania and New Jersey.

**H2: Jeffrey J. Devine — Founder & President**
Jeff Devine is a Licensed Master Plumber with more than two decades in the
trade — he started as a teenager and never left the industry. He founded
Plumbing Resolution in 2016 and has grown it into a roughly 40-person
contractor trusted by developers and general contractors across the
region. Jeff stays personally involved in the work: he oversees
operations, estimating, project management, safety compliance, workforce
development, and client relations — which is why clients tend to know
exactly who's accountable for their project.

Away from the job, Jeff is a husband and father of two, and is actively
involved in charitable and community organizations throughout the Greater
Philadelphia region.
